Our Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. Your privacy and security are our priorities.

- Last Updated on August, 2026

What and Why

This app does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. All data remains locally on your device.

We collect NO personal data because:

  • No account creation required
  • No login or registration process
  • No internet connection needed
  • No tracking or analytics tools
  • No third-party services
  • No advertisements

All user data — including study progress, quiz scores, saved questions, and app preferences — is stored ONLY on your device.

When you uninstall the app, all data is permanently removed from your device.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is zero risk of your data being intercepted, leaked, or misused.

We do not have access to your information — period.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Right to deletion: Uninstall the app to remove all data
Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

No external libraries that collect data. No SDKs with tracking capabilities. No advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Since we collect no data at all, there is nothing to collect or protect.
