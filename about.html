Privacy Policy

Exam Prep is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Last Updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information.

This app is designed to work completely offline. No data is ever transmitted to any server or third party.

  • No account registration required
  • No login or profile creation
  • No internet access needed for core functionality
  • No tracking cookies or analytics
  • No advertising networks
  • No data sharing with third parties
Data Storage

All data generated by your use of this app is stored locally on your device. This includes:

  • Study progress and quiz scores
  • Bookmarks and saved questions
  • App preferences (theme, notifications, etc.)
  • Practice test history

We never have access to this data. It remains solely on your device.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete all data: Uninstall the app — all data is permanently removed
  • Reset progress: Use the "Reset" option within the app settings
Data Security

🔒 Industry-Standard Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your device and data from unauthorized access. Since no data is transmitted or stored on our servers, your information remains completely under your control.

Key security features:

  • All data stays locally on your device
  • No network transmission of personal data
  • Zero third-party data access
  • No server-side storage
Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, all your information is fully in your control:

  • Right to Access: View all your data directly in the app
  • Right to Correction: Update your progress and preferences anytime
  • Right to Deletion: Uninstall the app to delete everything
  • Right to Data Portability: All data is stored locally in a format accessible to you
Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services for specific functionalities:

  • GitHub: Used exclusively for checking app updates. No personal data is collected or transmitted.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used for license activation and secure authentication. No personal data is collected or stored.

Neither service collects, stores, or shares personal information. All communications are encrypted and limited to essential functionality.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Since we collect no data at all, there is no risk of children's data being collected, stored, or shared.

Parents and guardians can be assured that this app is safe for their children to use.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available within this app.

If we make significant changes that affect your rights, we will notify you through the app.
